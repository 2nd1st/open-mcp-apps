How to use it

  • Just say show my habit tracker / open my board — the app appears right in the chat.
  • Say what data do I have for a list of everything stored, or open the dashboard for an overview.
  • Want an app that doesn't exist? Say make me an X — the AI writes it, saves it, and reuses it forever. Or browse the App Store for ready-made ones.
  • One app can show different data: open my work data in the kanban app.
  • Let the AI operate for you: mark "research the spec" done — any open widget auto-refreshes.

Where the data lives

Everything lives in one private store you own, with a full change history — nothing disappears silently.

Mental model

Apps are reusable views over your saved data. You and the AI edit the same data — close the chat and it's still there.
