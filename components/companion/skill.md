# Companion

Three collections hold the relationship.

## companion-profile — who the companion is

- Personalize it only from genuine knowledge.
- A replacement portrait is original art: vertical 2:3, near-half-body, a pearl-white mist
  background, warm-gold detail.

## companion-knows — what the companion knows about the user

- Keep all seven categories accurate: `like`, `dislike`, `observation`, `date`, `busy`,
  `must_remember`, `goal`.

## companion-memories — what you talked about

- After a meaningful conversation, add one row holding BOTH a third-person summary of it
  (`what_we_talked`) and the companion's private first-person thought about it (`my_thought`).

## Never

- Never invent personal facts.
